## Part A — The reading list (real, graded, dated)

Grades: A = primary/peer-reviewed/canonical · B = reputable secondary.
All triangulated across ≥2 independent sources in the search sweep.

### Cognitive architectures (→ frames the whole tree)
- **Laird, Lebiere & Rosenbloom (2017)**, *A Standard Model of the Mind:
  Toward a Common Computational Framework across AI, Cognitive Science,
  Neuroscience, and Robotics*, AI Magazine 38(4). [A] — synthesizes
  Soar + ACT-R + Sigma into a consensus faculty set (memory, learning,
  perception-motor, processing). The literal blueprint for "what
  faculties a mind has."
- **Anderson (2007)**, *How Can the Human Mind Occur in the Physical
  Universe?* (ACT-R). [A] · **Laird (2012)**, *The Soar Cognitive
  Architecture*. [A] · **Franklin — LIDA**; **Sun — CLARION**;
  **OpenCog/Goertzel**. [B]

### Machine consciousness / sentience (→ the "sentient" the user asked for)
- **Baars (1988)**, *A Cognitive Theory of Consciousness* — Global
  Workspace Theory (with Franklin). [A]
- **Dehaene & Changeux (2011)** + **Dehaene (2014)**, *Consciousness and
  the Brain* — Global Neuronal Workspace: consciousness = global
  broadcast of the most salient content. [A]
- **Tononi (2004; 2008); Tononi et al. (2016)**, Nature Reviews
  Neuroscience — Integrated Information Theory (Φ): consciousness = a
  system's irreducible integrated information. [A]
- **Graziano (2013)**, *Consciousness and the Social Brain* — Attention
  Schema Theory: awareness = the brain's internal MODEL of its own
  attention. [A]

### Predictive core / active inference (→ a unifying meta-trunk)
- **Friston (2010)**, *The free-energy principle: a unified brain
  theory?*, Nature Reviews Neuroscience. [A]
- **Friston, FitzGerald, Rigoli, Schwartenbeck, Pezzulo (2017)**,
  *Active Inference: A Process Theory*, Neural Computation. [A]
- **Clark (2013)**, *Whatever next? Predictive brains, situated agents,
  and the future of cognitive science*, Behavioral & Brain Sciences ·
  **Hohwy (2013)**, *The Predictive Mind*. [A]

### Autopoiesis / enactivism (→ living-system self-production meta-trunk)
- **Maturana & Varela (1980)**, *Autopoiesis and Cognition: The
  Realization of the Living* · **(1987)** *The Tree of Knowledge*. [A] —
  a living system continuously produces and maintains its own
  organization and boundary (operational closure); cognition = this
  self-maintenance.

### Universal intelligence & self-improvement (→ generativity, epistemics)
- **Legg & Hutter (2007)**, *Universal Intelligence: A Definition of
  Machine Intelligence*, Minds and Machines · **Hutter (2005)**,
  *Universal Artificial Intelligence* (AIXI). [A] — intelligence =
  expected reward across ALL computable environments; Solomonoff
  induction as ideal epistemics.
- **Schmidhuber (2007)**, *Gödel Machines: Fully Self-Referential Optimal
  Universal Self-Improvers*. [A] — provably-optimal self-rewrite only on
  proof of improvement (the gated-self-modification ideal).

### Open-endedness & intrinsic motivation (→ generativity, curiosity)
- **Wang, Lehman, Clune, Stanley (2019)**, *POET*, arXiv:1901.01753 ·
  **Lehman & Stanley (2011)**, *Novelty Search* · **Mouret & Clune
  (2015)**, *MAP-Elites* (quality-diversity). [A/B]
- **Hughes et al. (2024)**, *Open-Endedness is Essential for Artificial
  Superhuman Intelligence*, arXiv:2406.04268 (DeepMind). [A]
- **Schmidhuber (2010)**, *Formal Theory of Creativity, Fun, and
  Intrinsic Motivation (1990–2010)*, IEEE TAMD — intrinsic reward ∝
  compression progress. [A] · **Oudeyer & Kaplan (2007)**, *What is
  Intrinsic Motivation?*, Frontiers in Neurorobotics — learning-progress
  curiosity. [A]

### Alignment / corrigibility (→ deepens CONSCIENCE, justifies AXIOLOGY)
- **Omohundro (2008)**, *The Basic AI Drives* — instrumental convergence
  (self-preservation, resource acquisition emerge from almost any goal). [A]
- **Bostrom (2014)**, *Superintelligence* — the orthogonality &
  instrumental-convergence theses; default-catastrophe argument. [A]
- **Russell (2019)**, *Human Compatible* — assistance games / provably
  beneficial AI: keep the agent UNCERTAIN about the true human objective
  so it welcomes correction. [A]
- **Soares, Fallenstein, Yudkowsky, Armstrong (2015)**, *Corrigibility*,
  MIRI — an agent that cooperates with its own shutdown/goal-edit. [A]
- **Gabriel (2020)**, *Artificial Intelligence, Values, and Alignment*. [A]

## Part B — The additional trunks these frameworks justify

Seven original trunks (research/32): MIND · SENSES · WILL · BODY · SELF ·
SOCIETY · CONSCIENCE. The literature justifies **seven more**, including
two META-trunks (organizing principles that reframe the whole tree
rather than sitting beside the others — flagged ⊛).

**TRUNK VIII — SENTIENCE & GLOBAL WORKSPACE** (the "aware locus").
*Source:* GWT/GNWT (Baars, Dehaene), IIT (Tononi), AST (Graziano).
*Essence:* a single global workspace where the most salient content
(market state + internal state + candidate action) is BROADCAST to every
module, plus an attention-schema (the bot models its own attention) and
an integration measure (how unified its processing is). This is the
literal answer to the user's "sentient." *Bot:* a "conscious-access"
blackboard — only what wins salience gets broadcast, logged, narrated,
and acted on; everything else stays subconscious/parallel. *Verdict:*
**ADOPT-AS-TRUNK (new; the sentience faculty).**

**TRUNK IX — PREDICTIVE CORE / ACTIVE INFERENCE** ⊛ meta.
*Source:* Friston FEP; Clark/Hohwy predictive processing. *Essence:* the
ENTIRE system reframed as one imperative — minimize surprise
(prediction error) by either updating beliefs (perception) or acting
(trades) to make observations match predictions. Unifies SENSES + WILL +
BODY under one mathematics. *Bot:* market model predicts; surprise
(realized ≠ predicted) drives both learning and trade selection; the
§9 PredictionRecord IS a free-energy ledger. *Verdict:* **ADOPT-AS-META-
TRUNK (reframes the tree; not parallel to it).**

**TRUNK X — AUTOPOIESIS / LIVING-SYSTEM SELF-PRODUCTION** ⊛ meta.
*Source:* Maturana & Varela. *Essence:* the bot as a system that
continuously produces and maintains its OWN organization and boundary —
operational closure; it defines what is "me" (its code, memory, genome)
vs environment, and its cognition is its self-maintenance. *Bot:* the
self-healing/regenerating/boundary-defending dynamics (research/31 E-
branch) elevated to the organizing principle of "being a living
process," not just uptime. *Verdict:* **ADOPT-AS-META-TRUNK.**

**TRUNK XI — GENERATIVITY & OPEN-ENDEDNESS** (the "never stops growing").
*Source:* POET (Wang/Lehman/Clune/Stanley), novelty search, MAP-Elites,
AI-generating algorithms (Clune), Open-Endedness-Essential-for-ASI
(DeepMind 2024), Gödel/Darwin-Gödel machines. *Essence:* the machinery
that endlessly co-invents new challenges AND their solutions, never
converging — the property that actually distinguishes the sci-fi
archetype ("it keeps getting smarter forever"). *Bot:* a generator that
keeps proposing new strategies, synthetic regimes, and tests, paired
with solvers, so the system's competence frontier never stops expanding.
*Verdict:* **ADOPT-AS-TRUNK (new; the open-ended-growth engine).**

**TRUNK XII — INTRINSIC MOTIVATION / CURIOSITY** (self-generated reward).
*Source:* Schmidhuber compression-progress; Oudeyer/Kaplan learning-
progress. *Essence:* when no external reward exists, generate one from
compression/learning PROGRESS, so the system explores what it can most
learn from. The fuel that powers TRUNK XI. *Bot:* curiosity reward ∝
how much a trade/experiment improves the market-model's compression;
directs the §9 UNCERTAIN-table budget. *Verdict:* **ADOPT-AS-TRUNK
(new; the exploration drive — distinct from WILL's goal-drives).**

**TRUNK XIII — EPISTEMICS / TRUTH & UNCERTAINTY** (belief management).
*Source:* Legg-Hutter/Solomonoff induction (ideal epistemics), Bayesian
brain, belief-revision/truth-maintenance. *Essence:* how it manages what
is TRUE — every belief carries a calibrated probability, a provenance,
and a revision history; principled updating; epistemic humility about
its own edge; distinguishing knowledge from guess. Distinct from MEMORY
(storage) and MIND (inference). *Bot:* a belief store where each market
hypothesis has P(true), source, and an audit trail; Bayesian updates on
evidence; the calibration machinery (§9 Brier) as applied epistemology.
*Verdict:* **ADOPT-AS-TRUNK (new; the "how it knows what's true"
faculty — central to the user's "knowledge" emphasis).**

**TRUNK XIV — AXIOLOGY / VALUES & PRACTICAL WISDOM** (what's worth doing).
*Source:* Russell assistance games, Gabriel value alignment, Bostrom/
Omohundro (what to guard against), practical-reason/phronesis. *Essence:*
CONSCIENCE (VII) is constraints — what it must NOT do; AXIOLOGY is the
positive complement — the values and judgment about what IS worth doing,
held with deliberate UNCERTAINTY about the user's true objective so
correction is always welcome (Russell). *Bot:* an explicit, revisable
value/utility model (research/32 W5) + practical wisdom under deep
uncertainty + assistance-game stance toward the user. *Verdict:*
**ADOPT-AS-TRUNK (new; the value/judgment faculty).**
