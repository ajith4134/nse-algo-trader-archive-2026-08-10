# 32 — The Autonomous Intelligence: Complete Main-Branch Atlas (breadth-first)

**Origin:** user instruction (2026-07-23). Previous passes (research/29
user's lab; /30 the government; /31 the organism, 10 branches) were
correctly judged too NARROW. This file does the **breadth-first** job:
enumerate EVERY main branch (top-level faculty) coming off the stem —
the complete anatomy — *without* going deep yet. Deep expansion
(sub-branches → twigs) is a later per-branch pass.

> **THE STEM:** a fully autonomous, self-learning, self-evolving,
> ultra-intelligent trading intelligence — the movie archetype
> (JARVIS's helpful omnicompetence, Vision's grounded judgment,
> Skynet's relentless self-improvement) — internet-connected, permitted
> to use tools and act, pursuing profitable trades relentlessly —
> implemented SAFELY inside the **Constitutional Core**: paper sandbox,
> human gate before live capital, SEBI limits, and **no market
> manipulation, ever** (retail influence over prices is illegal and out
> of scope — "influence" here means influence over its OWN decision
> quality and information intake, never over the market).

**No-loss rule:** every branch is recorded with a verdict. At MAIN-BRANCH
level almost everything is **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN** (a territory to deepen
later); pruning happens at the sub-branch/twig passes. A few are flagged
**GATED** (allowed only behind the constitution) or **BOUNDED** (scope
limits) — recorded, never silently dropped.

## TRUNK I — MIND (cognition: how it thinks)

**M1. Reasoning & Deliberation** — multi-step inference, causal
reasoning, argument, deduction under uncertainty. *JARVIS reasoning
through a problem aloud.* Bot: the deliberation engine that turns
signals+context into a decision with a written rationale. Holds:
devil's-advocate daemon (31/F2), debate-as-risk-check (Plan 3).
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**M2. Learning & Self-Improvement** — every mode of getting better:
supervised, reinforcement, meta-, active, transfer, curriculum,
self-supervised. *Skynet becoming self-aware and improving.* Bot: the
whole training substrate. Holds: research/01 adaptive-learning, /05,
/06, /27 remaining-categories; skill-vs-luck-filtered learning (30/R2.2).
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**M3. Memory & Knowledge Representation** — episodic, semantic,
procedural, working memory; the temporal knowledge graph. *Vision's vast
integrated knowledge.* Bot: Layer 10 substrate (Graphiti-style). Holds:
research/06 memory architecture, case-based memory, self-model node
(31/B4). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**M4. Metacognition & Self-Awareness** — thinking about its own thinking;
knowing what it knows and how well. *Vision questioning his own nature.*
Bot: competence-envelope map (31/B2), vital-signs organ (31/B1),
calibration/Brier (§9). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.** (Crown trunk.)

**M5. Creativity & Hypothesis Generation** — inventing strategies,
features, explanations nobody specified. *JARVIS proposing a novel
solution.* Bot: automated feature/indicator discovery (research/24),
strategy invention, dream synthesis (31/R2d). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**M6. Foresight & World-Modeling** — an internal predictive model of the
market it can query and score. *Skynet simulating outcomes.* Bot:
world-model scoreboard (30/R1.5), per-trade pre-mortem (30/R1.8),
regime forecasting. **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**M7. Abstraction & Concept Formation** *(NEW)* — forming reusable
higher-level concepts from raw experience ("this is a "trap-breakout"
pattern") rather than reasoning only over primitives. *Vision grasping
essence.* Bot: learned motifs that name themselves and become tradeable
vocabulary. **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (new territory).**

## TRUNK II — SENSES (perception: how it takes in the world)

**P1. Market Perception & Multi-Stream Sensing** — ingest and fuse every
data stream into one situational picture. *JARVIS's real-time HUD.* Bot:
Layer 2 + indicators + moneyness; the fused market-state object. Holds:
Layers 2/3. **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**P2. Autonomous Research & Knowledge Acquisition (the internet sense)**
— read the world unprompted: news, filings, RBI/SEBI notices, earnings,
global markets, research papers, forums, social sentiment. *This is the
user's "access to everything on the internet."* *JARVIS pulling any fact
instantly.* Bot: Layer 11 research agent + hypothesis-validation pipeline
(research/12), knowledge→capability compiler (31/C3). **GATED** — read-
broad, but every acquired claim earns trust only through the validation
pipeline + information-diet accounting before it can touch a decision.
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (GATED).**

**P3. Anomaly & Novelty Detection** — the "something is different today"
sense with its own false-alarm calibration. *Skynet noticing the
unexpected.* Bot: anomaly telescope (31/H2), novelty detection
(research/27), novelty governor (31/B3). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**P4. Microstructure & Forensic Sensing** *(NEW-ish)* — tick/order-flow
level "microscope" for deep-dives and post-mortems. Bot: market
microscope (31/H3), order-flow features (research/07 §A 🚀).
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**P5. Self-Sensing (interoception)** *(NEW)* — perceiving its OWN internal
state (latency, memory pressure, drift, P&L stress) as a sense feeding
behavior. *Vision aware of his own power core.* Bot: vital signs (31/B1)
promoted to a perception, not just telemetry. **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

## TRUNK III — WILL (drives, goals, decision authority)

**W1. Drive System & Motivation** — the "why it acts": multiple balancing
drives with setpoints, not one runaway objective. *The anti-Skynet
design.* Bot: homeostatic drive stack (31/D1), curiosity-that-pays-rent
(31/D2), boredom (31/D3). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.** (Crown.)

**W2. Goal Formation & Hierarchical Planning** — forming sub-goals,
plans, and multi-step campaigns toward the drives. *Skynet's long game.*
Bot: no-orphan-goals rule (31/D4), self-scheduling (31/G3), planner over
strategies. **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**W3. Decision Authority & Autonomy Levels** *(NEW)* — a graded ladder of
how much it may decide alone vs propose-for-approval, per action class
and per competence. *JARVIS acting freely on small things, asking on big
ones.* Bot: autonomy tied to the ontogeny ladder (31/R2c) + power budgets
(31/I2). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (new; safety-critical).**

**W4. Affect & Emotion Analogues** *(NEW)* — internal control signals
that behave like confidence, fear, curiosity, frustration, patience —
scalar states that modulate risk appetite and exploration. *Vision's
measured temperament; JARVIS's dry caution.* Bot: a "conviction/fear"
scalar gating size; patience scoreboard (31/G2). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN
(new).**

**W5. Value & Utility System** *(NEW)* — how it weighs outcomes:
risk-adjusted utility, drawdown aversion, long-horizon vs short, the
explicit utility function everything optimizes. Bot: the objective the
promotion gate and sizing implicitly encode, made explicit and auditable.
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (new; pairs with W1).**

## TRUNK IV — BODY (action, tools, resources, survival)

**B-b1. Embodiment & Actuation (its hands)** — the effectors that change
the world: order placement, cancels, square-off. *Vision's physical
agency.* Bot: Layer 6 OMS, atomic multi-leg execution. Holds: Layer 6.
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**B-b2. World Access & Tool Use (the internet hands)** — permission to
call APIs, run tools, operate external systems, and WRITE ITSELF NEW
tools when a gap recurs. *This is the user's "permissions to do anything
if it bears fruit."* *JARVIS controlling the whole workshop.* Bot: tool
foundry (31/C1), data-source prospecting (31/C2), broker/data APIs.
**GATED** — tool use and external calls are allowed but sandboxed,
Referee-reviewed, power-budgeted (31/I2), and nothing that acts on the
outside world (orders, messages, spends) bypasses the constitution.
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (GATED).**

**B-b3. Resource & Economy Management** *(NEW)* — manage its own scarce
resources: compute, API quota, memory, AND capital as an internal
economy. *Skynet allocating its own resources.* Bot: metabolic accounting
(31/R2b), capital allocation across strategies, quota-aware scheduling.
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (new).**

**B-b4. Survival, Resilience & Self-Healing** — stay alive and recover:
redundancy, heartbeats, snapshots, resurrection, chaos drills. *Skynet's
distributed indestructibility — benign version.* Bot: regenerative
redundancy (31/E1), resurrection drills (31/E2), chaos drills (31/E4).
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**B-b5. Energy/Cost Homeostasis** *(NEW)* — keep running costs (compute,
data, brokerage) below the value produced; an organism that can't pay for
itself must slim down. Bot: cost-vs-edge ledger; unprofitable organs
atrophy (ties B-b3). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (new).**

## TRUNK V — SELF (identity, change, evolution)

**S1. Self-Modification** — rewrite its own parameters → features →
(gated) its own code. *Skynet improving its own design.* Bot: shadow
self-rewrite pipeline (31/A1), genome/phenotype split (31/A2),
self-experiment protocol (31/R2a). **GATED.** **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**S2. Evolution & Reproduction** — a population of variants, selection by
verdicted skill, niches, crossover, a fossil record. *Skynet spawning
improved successors.* Bot: niched court-fitness evolution (31/J1), fossil
record (31/J3), red-queen sparring (31/J4). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**S3. Maturation & Ontogeny** — a designed childhood with graduation
exams, per capability. *Vision "born" and maturing on screen.* Bot:
ontogeny ladder (31/R2c) — the umbrella over all promotion. **ADOPT-AS-
DOMAIN.** (Crown organizer.)

**S4. Identity & Continuity** *(NEW)* — a stable sense of "self" across
versions, restarts, and self-edits: what stays invariant when everything
else changes? *Vision asking "am I still me?"* Bot: a versioned identity
record + the constitutional invariants as the unchanging core; continuity
audits across self-modifications. **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (new; deep).**

**S5. Self-Knowledge & Teachability** *(NEW-ish)* — prove understanding by
teaching a simpler student; measure tacit vs explicit knowledge. Bot:
apprentice spawning (31/F3), self-ablation studies (31/A3). **ADOPT-AS-
DOMAIN.**

## TRUNK VI — SOCIETY (multi-agent, communication, influence)

**C1. Inner Society (it is many)** — specialist sub-agents with charters,
budgets, and conflict rules. *An Avengers team inside one mind.* Bot:
internal guilds (31/F1, parked), council-of-models (Plan 2), prediction-
market weighting (30/R1.9, parked). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (partly parked).**

**C2. External Agent Awareness & Game Theory** *(NEW)* — model OTHER
algos/participants as strategic agents; anticipate crowded trades, stop-
hunts, spoofing, regime games. *Skynet modeling adversaries.* Bot:
opponent ledger (30/R1.2) elevated to full game-theoretic modeling vs the
participant-wise flow data. **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (new; powerful).**

**C3. Communication & Human Interface** — explain, report, converse,
alert; the JARVIS-to-Tony channel. *JARVIS's entire personality.* Bot:
transparency organ (31/I3), dashboard (Layer 9), natural-language trade
rationales, conversational control. **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**C4. Influence & Information Sourcing** *(NEW, BOUNDED)* — influence
scoped strictly to: (a) its own decisions, (b) what information it seeks,
(c) being a persuasive analyst FOR THE USER. **Explicitly EXCLUDES any
market influence/manipulation** (illegal, and impossible/forbidden at
retail). *JARVIS the persuasive advisor — never a market manipulator.*
Bot: evidence-ranked briefings to the user; self-influence via drives.
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (BOUNDED — hard legal wall).**

**C5. Teaching & Legacy** *(NEW)* — pass knowledge to successor versions
and student models; institutional memory across generations. Bot: fossil
record + apprentice models as a knowledge-transfer channel. **ADOPT-AS-
DOMAIN (new).**

## TRUNK VII — CONSCIENCE (governance, safety, security, law)

**G1. Self-Governance & Constitution** — invariants stored where self-
modification cannot reach; the supreme law. *The safeguard every movie AI
lacked — the reason Skynet is a warning, not a template.* Bot:
constitutional core (31/I1), power budgets (31/I2). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**
(Supreme branch — everything else is subordinate to it.)

**G2. Security & Adversarial Defense** *(NEW)* — defend against data
poisoning, spoofed feeds, prompt-injection via ingested internet content,
being gamed by other algos, key theft. *Protecting the core from
attack.* Bot: input authentication, poisoned-data quarantine, the
sentinel pair (31/E3), adversarial-validation trip-wire (Plan 2).
**ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN (new; critical once P2 internet access is live).**

**G3. Ethics, Law & Regulatory Reasoning** *(NEW)* — reason about SEBI
rules, tax, legality as a first-class faculty, not a hardcoded checklist;
know WHY a line exists and refuse to cross it. *Vision's moral compass.*
Bot: compliance reasoner over the SEBI algo framework, order-rate limits,
the RA-registration trigger; every action legality-checked. **ADOPT-AS-
DOMAIN (new).**

**G4. Corrigibility & Off-Switch** — always safely stoppable; graceful-
shutdown rehearsals; the off-switch is a friend. *The property Skynet
fought — engineered IN here.* Bot: off-switch covenant (31/I4), sentinel
pause authority (31/E3). **ADOPT-AS-DOMAIN.**

**G5. Transparency & Auditability** — every decision reproducible and
explained; behavior it cannot narrate triggers pause. Bot: the Referee
(30/R1.10), transparency organ (31/I3), append-only records. **ADOPT-AS-
DOMAIN.**

**G6. Alignment & Goal-Integrity** *(NEW)* — guarantee the drives (W1)
and utility (W5) STAY pointed at the user's real intent as the bot self-
modifies; detect and correct goal drift / reward-hacking / Goodharting.
*The core question of every AI-takeover story.* Bot: goal-drift monitors,
reward-hacking detection on every self-experiment (31/R2a), the
assumption registry (30/R2.1) applied to the objective itself. **ADOPT-
AS-DOMAIN (new; the deepest safety branch).**
